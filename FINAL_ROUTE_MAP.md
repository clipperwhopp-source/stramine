The root `/` is a full English homepage. Unlocalized `/apps/`, `/about/`, `/support/`, `/privacy/`, `/contact/` and app-specific routes use static meta-refresh fallbacks to their English equivalents; they are excluded from the sitemap and marked `noindex`. `/404.html` is a custom page for GitHub Pages. `/sitemap.xml` and `/robots.txt` are at the production prefix.

These STRAMINE policy pages are **directories linking to each app's existing complete policy**, not replacement legal text. WallPair's pairing endpoint and association files remain solely on `wallpair.pages.dev`.
